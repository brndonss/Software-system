# Northstar Product Demo Script

## Production Direction

**Approximate duration:** 75 seconds

**Narrator:** Professional adult woman. Warm, calm, confident, intelligent, and trustworthy. Natural conversational pacing with a modern product-film tone. The delivery should feel observant and precise, never breathless or aggressively sales-driven.

**Visual language:** Near-black product UI, off-white type, restrained green signals, thin connections, and clear review states. Every transition should explain a change in the business.

## Scene 1 — A Business Is Operating

**Timing:** 0:00–0:09

**Visual:** The Business System opens quietly around a central Business card. Customers, Leads, Tasks, Workflows, Automations, and Activity are visible but subdued. A small green signal rests at the center.

**Narration:**

> Running a business creates signals everywhere.
>
> Customers, leads, tasks, follow-ups, and decisions are already part of the work.

## Scene 2 — Something Happens

**Timing:** 0:09–0:18

**Visual:** A new lead enters the network.

```text
NEW LEAD
Illustrative Company
Landscape project
Website
Just now
```

The Leads node activates and a restrained green signal travels along the existing connection.

**Narration:**

> A new lead comes in.
>
> But receiving the lead is only the beginning.

## Scene 3 — Context Connects

**Timing:** 0:18–0:29

**Visual:** The signal connects the lead to its surrounding context. Show the lead source, status, customer information, and business context becoming visible. The AI state moves through:

```text
Reading business context...
Checking lead status...
Identifying next action...
READY TO RECOMMEND
```

**Narration:**

> The system connects that signal with the context already inside your business.
>
> Not to make a decision in the dark, but to make the next one easier to see.

## Scene 4 — The Next Action

**Timing:** 0:29–0:40

**Visual:** Surrounding cards soften. The recommendation becomes the focal point.

```text
RECOMMENDED NEXT ACTION
Follow up with new lead

WHY THIS?
New lead
Website source
No response recorded

SUGGESTED
Create follow-up task

[ Review ]
```

**Narration:**

> So instead of asking what should happen next, you get a clear recommendation.
>
> The reason stays visible, and the decision stays with you.

## Scene 5 — From Decision to Work

**Timing:** 0:40–0:51

**Visual:** The recommendation connects to a task and then a workflow.

```text
FOLLOW UP
Illustrative Company
Today · High priority

NEW LEAD FOLLOW-UP
Lead received → Review → Follow up → Track result
```

The connecting lines brighten briefly as the signal moves through the states.

**Narration:**

> Turn that decision into organized work.
>
> A task. A follow-up. A repeatable workflow the team can review and use.

## Scene 6 — Automation With Control

**Timing:** 0:51–1:03

**Visual:** An automation recommendation appears.

```text
AUTOMATION RECOMMENDED
New Lead Follow-up

WHEN
New lead needs follow-up

ACTION
Prepare next step

STATUS
Recommended

[ Review ] [ Approve ]
```

The illustrative status transitions from `Recommended` to `Approved` to `Active` only as a visual product sequence. No backend action is implied.

**Narration:**

> When work becomes repeatable, the system can recommend an automation.
>
> Review it. Approve it when it fits. Stay in control.

## Scene 7 — Every Action Stays Visible

**Timing:** 1:03–1:13

**Visual:** The final signal enters Activity.

```text
ACTIVITY
Lead received                 9:41 AM
Context analyzed              9:41 AM
Follow-up recommended         9:42 AM
Task created                  9:42 AM
Automation approved           9:43 AM
```

**Narration:**

> Every action stays visible.
>
> So the business has a record of what happened, what was recommended, and what was approved.

## Scene 8 — The Connected System

**Timing:** 1:13–1:25

**Visual:** The camera pulls back to the full Business System. Business is central, with Customers, Leads, Context, Recommendations, Tasks, Workflows, Automations, and Activity connected. One slow green signal moves through a single connection, then rests.

**Narration:**

> From business context to business action.
>
> One connected system for the work behind your business.

**On-screen close:**

```text
One system
Every part of your business

Get started for free
```

## Voice Notes

- Pause briefly after each major product state.
- Give “Why this?” and “Stay in control” a little extra space.
- Keep the final line grounded and matter-of-fact.
- Do not imply autonomous AI employees, automatic publishing, unsupported integrations, or unreviewed automation execution.
